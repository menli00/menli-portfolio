My study diary

1. My first C program

When: At the start of the semester.

What I learned: The basics of C from YouTube videos, and I wrote my first program, a calculator.

The problem: In my first lab class I understood almost nothing, because I had never studied C. Later my calculator would not run, and for a whole hour I could not find the reason.

How I solved it: At home I learned the basics from videos. Then I found the reason: I had forgotten a semicolon. After I fixed it, the calculator worked, and I was really happy.

2. Arrays lab (实验5)

When: Later in the semester.

What I learned: How to work with arrays in C.

The problem: Some of my programs had errors.

How I solved it: I went through the code, found where the mistakes were and corrected them. Then I wrote a lab report that explains each fix and shows the correct code.

3. Pointers lab (实验6)

When: Later in the semester.

What I learned: How pointers work in C. I worked on nine programs and ran them in VS Code.

The problem: Some programs needed corrections before they gave the right output.

How I solved it: I fixed the code and wrote a report with every correct program and a simple explanation of what I changed.

Since then I do all my coding tasks on my own, on EduCoder and in lab classes.
