About Me

Welcome to My World

Hi, I'm Mengli. I come from Turkmenistan, and now I live and study in Wuhan, China.

I study at Huazhong University of Science and Technology. I chose this university and my major because I think it is a very strong university, and I wanted to learn in a place that takes technical subjects seriously.

My dream is to become a developer. I like code, logic and math, and I want to build useful things one day.

I also love drawing and design. I like making pictures and playing with colors and fonts, and this website is part of that. From time to time I also bake something tasty.

Photography, Wuhan and China

Taking photos is my favorite hobby. I like to look for beautiful places and catch them on camera: a lake in the evening, a path between trees, a cloudy sky before the rain.

I really love the nature of Wuhan. What I like most is that one city has both nature and big modern buildings. I often walk around East Lake, and every time the view is a little different.

I am also very interested in Chinese culture. Living here, I learn something new every day: new words, new food, new places. I try to see as much as I can and take photos of it.

And I adore animals. When I meet one, I always want to take a photo.
